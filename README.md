🏠 Homepage
The Shopora homepage provides the main entry point into the marketplace, combining product discovery, category navigation, responsive navigation, and promotional content in a single shopping experience. The navbar includes quick product search, while the hero section features a prominent search interface that helps users discover products immediately.

❤️ Favorites & Saved Products
Shopora allows customers to save products they are interested in and manage them from a dedicated favorites page. Saved products can be reviewed at any time, giving customers an easy way to keep track of items they may want to purchase later and quickly return them to their shopping journey.

🛒 Shopping Cart
The Shopora shopping cart brings selected products together in one place, allowing customers to review their items, adjust quantities, remove products, and see the order summary before proceeding to checkout.
<img width="956" height="436" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/90801b75-a3e9-40b9-9746-2a1511413fe4" />

💳 Secure Checkout & Paystack Payments
Shopora integrates Paystack to provide a complete checkout and payment experience. The project uses Paystack's demo/test environment, allowing the payment flow to be safely demonstrated from checkout through transaction completion without processing real customer payments.
<img width="936" height="422" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6234919c-bd4f-4ad2-aa78-16063682ac72" />

📦 Order Management & Tracking
After completing a purchase, customers can access their order history and review individual orders, including purchased products, order details, payment information, and current order status. This provides a clear post-purchase experience from checkout through order management.

The Shopora footer provides quick access to important areas of the marketplace, supporting navigation beyond the main shopping interface.
